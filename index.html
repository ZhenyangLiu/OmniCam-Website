03 / THE BIG PICTURE

From understanding a scene
to moving through it.

One model connects camera intent with scene geometry, across generation, reconstruction, and active perception.

THE IDEA

Camera motion is more than a path through space. It is a decision about where to move, how to orient, and what to attend to. OmniCam brings panoramic geometry and language together in an autoregressive model, with hybrid pose tokens and explicit 3D target anchoring.
